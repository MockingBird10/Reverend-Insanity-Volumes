# Reverend-Insanity-Volumes
I am making custom volumes of Reverend Insanity for myself

Reverend_Insanity.epub is the ebook version of the novel translation and 蛊真人 - 蛊真人.epub is the novel in its original chinese language.

I used Calibre software to split the books by volume and this can bee seen in the folder Gu Zhen Ren.

My finalised version of Volume 1 is named with (Editted).

All illustrations are made by @Ullassseu

I have 're-translated' the novel thanks to Deepseek AI, my process was submitting each chapter in english and the same chapter in the original chinese and fixing any translation errors and providing footnotes for better understanding. This took a LONG time and is currently the only method i know.
